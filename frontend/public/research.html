Why these models?

Benchmarks run in October 2026 on Napirajz strips (Hungarian, hand-lettered) plus Pepper&Carrot pages and meme captions (English → Hungarian). Goal: the cheapest model per step that is as good as Claude Sonnet or better.

  • The scene step is where cheap models break: they get the speaker wrong (who says which bubble). gemini-3.8-flash is close to Opus there.
  • The review pass (critique, then rewrite) adds about +0.7 points for strong models and makes weak ones worse, so it stays on with gemini.

  • One translation call for all bubbles: jokes and verbal tics span bubbles, splitting would resend the images and the scene every time.

Bubble detection in the browser

  • The small int8 detector (11 MB) runs on WASM: ~1 s per panel single-threaded on a desktop, byte-identical boxes run to run.
  • WebGPU was ~3× slower for this model (int8 and TopK ops fall back to the CPU) and drifts a few boxes, so WASM is the default.
  • Compared with the larger server model it finds speech bubbles equally well and misses some tiny captions and signs.

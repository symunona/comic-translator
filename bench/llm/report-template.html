Your question

Is PaddleOCR plus one Sonnet-class call enough?

Yes. Paddle alone is not usable for Hungarian: CER 0.17 and only a third of the accents survive. Give its draft to one model call that also sees the close-ups and the errors disappear. The draft matters most for weak models: Gemini Flash-Lite drops from 0.070 to 0.009 with it. Strong models read the close-ups fine without it. Without the close-ups (panel images only), every model gets worse, because accents are small.

Your question

Batching: one call or many?

Scene, translate and review were already one call per strip with every bubble in it; that is how the translator keeps running jokes and voices consistent. OCR was one call per panel. Reading the whole strip in one call is cheaper (images and rules are sent once) and for most models more accurate, so it is now the default (-ocr-per-panel keeps the old way). Sonnet is the exception (one box skipped), so prod keeps per-panel while it runs Sonnet.

How it was measured

Method and caveats

Data

  • Napirajz strips from the vault (kristalyhologram, zsenialis_rendszer, hatos_csavar, ket_nagy_szatyor; az_izzo_liget and vodorre for end to end).
  • Pepper&Carrot episode 6, pages 1–2 (CC-BY 4.0, David Revoy) in English, with the official Hungarian edition as reference.
  • Two English memes from the vault (vim.jpg, my computer.jpg).
  • The pipeline used to assume every comic was Hungarian. Strips now carry a source language (picker on upload), and the Napirajz hints only apply to Hungarian.

Scoring

  • OCR: CER after uppercasing and collapsing spaces; boxes that duplicate another box or are illegible were left out (4).
  • Judges: blind, candidates shuffled, panel images included, scores 1–10. Opus via the Claude CLI and Gemini 3.8 Flash; each favours its own family slightly.
  • Unreviewed drafts were judged alongside the final versions to measure what the review call buys.

Did not work

  • Qwen3.7-Plus: its only provider (Alibaba) aborts with an error on profanity-heavy strips; empty replies on 2 of 3 Napirajz strips.
  • Qwen3.8 Flash: rate-limited and very slow (long reasoning); CER 0.05–0.14.
  • PaddleOCR v6: worse than v5 latin on hand lettering; Tesseract: noisy.
  • Local LLMs on devbox's CPU: correct direction, ~10 minutes per strip.
